## Financial Methodologies and Frameworks

**Primary Z-Score Approaches**:
1. **Traditional Altman Z-Score**: Original 5-factor model for manufacturing companies
   - Formula: Z = 1.2×X₁ + 1.4×X₂ + 3.3×X₃ + 0.6×X₄ + 1.0×X₅
2. **Novel Retail Z-Score**: Enhanced 6-factor model with inventory component
   - Formula: Z = 1.2×X₁ + 1.4×X₂ + 3.3×X₃ + 0.6×X₄ + 1.0×X₅ + 0.998×X₆
3. **Service Company Model**: Adapted for technology and service-oriented businesses
   - Modified weightings to account for asset-light business models

- **Temporal Analysis**: Track Z-Score trends over multiple quarters/years
