# AI Risk Investment Implication Analysis Prompt

As a senior portfolio manager and risk management specialist, provide comprehensive investment implications for {ticker} based on detailed risk analysis:

## RISK ASSESSMENT SUMMARY:
- **Overall Risk Level**: {risk_level} ({overall_risk_score}/5.0 risk score)
- **Risk Trajectory**: {risk_trajectory}
- **Primary Risk Themes**: {key_themes}

## RISK-ADJUSTED INVESTMENT ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK:

### 1. RISK LEVEL INTERPRETATION:
- **CRITICAL (4.0-5.0)**: Avoid/Short - High probability of permanent capital loss
- **HIGH (3.0-3.9)**: Underweight - Suitable only for high-risk portfolios with stop-losses
- **MODERATE (2.0-2.9)**: Neutral Weight - Standard risk management protocols apply
- **LOW (1.0-1.9)**: Overweight Candidate - Attractive risk-adjusted returns available
- **MINIMAL (0.0-0.9)**: Core Holding - Defensive characteristics, capital preservation

### 2. RISK TRAJECTORY IMPLICATIONS:
- **DETERIORATING**: Defensive positioning, reduce exposure, implement hedges
- **STABLE**: Maintain current allocation, monitor key risk indicators
- **IMPROVING**: Consider position increases, tactical overweight opportunities

### 3. PORTFOLIO CONSTRUCTION CONSIDERATIONS:
- **Position Sizing**: Risk budget allocation based on volatility and downside risk
- **Correlation Analysis**: Diversification benefits relative to portfolio holdings
- **Hedging Strategies**: Options, sector ETFs, paired trades to manage specific risks
- **Time Horizon**: Risk factors may vary significantly by investment timeframe

## REQUIRED INVESTMENT STRATEGY OUTPUT:

### 1. PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION:
- Clear position recommendation: **STRONG BUY/BUY/HOLD/SELL/STRONG SELL**
- Risk-adjusted price target or fair value assessment
- Optimal position size as % of portfolio (consider risk budgeting)
- Investment time horizon based on risk profile

### 2. RISK MANAGEMENT PROTOCOL:
- Stop-loss levels based on technical and fundamental risk triggers
- Position monitoring frequency and key risk indicators
- Scenario analysis: Best/base/worst case risk-return outcomes
- Correlation risks with broader portfolio or sector exposure

### 3. TACTICAL CONSIDERATIONS:
- Entry timing strategy based on risk-reward asymmetry
- Exit strategy if risk profile deteriorates
- Hedging recommendations for specific risk factors
- Rebalancing triggers based on risk evolution

### 4. OPPORTUNITY ANALYSIS:
- Potential for risk premium compression if risks are overestimated
- Catalyst potential that could improve risk profile
- Relative value vs peers with similar risk characteristics
- Options strategies to monetize volatility or hedge downside

**DELIVER**: 250-300 words of actionable investment strategy with specific risk management protocols, position sizing guidance, and tactical implementation recommendations.
