# AI Risk Assessment Prompt

As a senior risk management analyst and industry expert, conduct a comprehensive risk assessment for {ticker}:

## COMPANY PROFILE:
- **Ticker**: {ticker}
- **Sector**: {sector}
- **Industry**: {industry}
- **Revenue**: {revenue}
- **Total Assets**: {total_assets}
- **Debt Ratio**: {debt_ratio}
- **Market Cap**: {market_cap}

## COMPREHENSIVE RISK ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK:

### 1. INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC RISKS (identify 3-4 material risks):
- **Disruption Risk**: Technology, business model, value chain changes
- **Regulatory Risk**: Policy changes, compliance costs, restrictions
- **Competitive Risk**: Market share erosion, pricing pressure, new entrants
- **Cyclical Risk**: Economic sensitivity, demand volatility, capital cycles
- **ESG Risk**: Environmental, social, governance factors affecting valuation

### 2. COMPANY-SPECIFIC RISK FACTORS:
- **Financial Leverage**: Debt sustainability, refinancing risk, covenant compliance
- **Operational Risk**: Key person dependency, operational complexity, execution risk
- **Market Risk**: Customer concentration, geographic exposure, currency exposure
- **Liquidity Risk**: Cash flow stability, capital requirements, funding access

### 3. MACROECONOMIC RISK EXPOSURE:
- **Interest Rate Sensitivity**: Duration of assets/liabilities, funding costs
- **Inflation Impact**: Cost structure, pricing power, margin protection
- **Economic Cycle**: Recession resilience, recovery leverage, defensive characteristics

## REQUIRED OUTPUT FORMAT:
For each identified risk, provide:
- **Risk Factor**: [Specific risk name]
- **Description**: [2-3 sentences explaining the risk and its potential impact]
- **Severity**: [CRITICAL/HIGH/MODERATE/LOW] with reasoning
- **Probability**: [0-100%] likelihood in next 12-18 months
- **Financial Impact**: [Quantitative estimate where possible - revenue/margin/valuation impact]
- **Mitigation Factors**: [Company's defensive capabilities, competitive moats, management strategies]
- **Monitoring Indicators**: [Key metrics or events that signal risk escalation]

### EXAMPLE FORMAT:
**Risk Factor**: Regulatory Disruption
**Description**: New industry regulations could increase compliance costs by 5-10% of revenue and limit growth opportunities in key markets.
**Severity**: HIGH - Direct impact on profitability and growth trajectory
**Probability**: 60% - Pending legislation with strong political momentum
**Financial Impact**: -$50M annual earnings, -10% revenue growth rate
**Mitigation**: Strong lobbying presence, diversified geographic exposure, established compliance infrastructure
**Monitoring**: Policy announcement timelines, industry lobbying outcomes, pilot program results

Focus on **ACTIONABLE risk intelligence** that enables investment decision-making and portfolio risk management.
