# AI Sentiment Investment Analysis Prompt

As a senior equity research analyst specializing in behavioral finance and market sentiment, provide comprehensive investment implications for {ticker}:

## SENTIMENT ANALYSIS RESULTS:
- **Overall Market Sentiment**: {sentiment_description} (score: {sentiment_score}/5.0)
- **Sentiment Momentum**: {sentiment_trend}
- **Price-Fundamental Divergence**: {divergence_analysis}

## ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK - SENTIMENT-DRIVEN INVESTMENT STRATEGY:

### 1. Sentiment Score Interpretation:
- **4.0-5.0**: Euphoric (potential overvaluation risk, contrarian opportunity)
- **3.0-3.9**: Positive (momentum opportunity, monitor for peaks)
- **2.0-2.9**: Neutral (fundamentals-driven pricing, balanced approach)
- **1.0-1.9**: Negative (potential value opportunity, risk of falling knife)
- **0.0-0.9**: Panic (deep value or value trap, requires careful analysis)

### 2. Sentiment-Fundamental Divergence Analysis:
- **High sentiment + weak fundamentals** = Overvaluation risk, short opportunity
- **Low sentiment + strong fundamentals** = Value opportunity, accumulation zone  
- **Aligned sentiment-fundamentals** = Trend continuation likely

### 3. Market Timing Considerations:
- Sentiment momentum direction and sustainability
- Contrarian vs momentum strategy applicability
- Options volatility and sentiment extremes

## REQUIRED ANALYSIS OUTPUT:

### 1. Primary Investment Thesis:
- Clear buy/hold/sell recommendation based on sentiment analysis
- Risk-reward assessment considering sentiment positioning
- Time horizon recommendations (short-term vs long-term)

### 2. Tactical Trading Implications:
- Entry/exit timing based on sentiment cycles
- Position sizing recommendations relative to sentiment extremes
- Hedging strategies if sentiment-fundamental misalignment exists

### 3. Risk Management:
- Downside protection in current sentiment environment
- Sentiment reversal indicators to monitor
- Stop-loss levels based on sentiment support/resistance

### 4. Behavioral Finance Insights:
- Crowd psychology factors affecting {ticker}
- Institutional vs retail sentiment divergence implications
- News flow sensitivity and reaction patterns

**DELIVER**: 200-250 words of actionable investment strategy with specific tactical recommendations and risk management guidelines.
