4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Applying turnaround theory, Wipro's position in the Distress zone necessitates urgent strategic action focused on stabilizing liquidity, reducing leverage, and restoring profitability. According to Hofer (1980), early intervention—such as cost restructuring and asset optimization—is critical at this stage to prevent further deterioration.

Given recent management changes and operational initiatives, a staged turnaround approach is advisable:
- Immediate: Focus on liquidity preservation, creditor negotiations, and cost containment.
- Mid-term: Implement strategic restructuring, divest non-core assets, and enhance operational efficiency.
- Long-term: Invest in innovation and digital transformation to rebuild competitive advantage, aligning with Hoskisson et al. (2004).

The company's current trajectory suggests a stabilization phase, but without significant improvement in Z-Score or liquidity metrics, the risk of failure remains high. The renewal process should prioritize strengthening working capital, reducing debt, and improving margins to shift the Z-Score into the Grey zone.
