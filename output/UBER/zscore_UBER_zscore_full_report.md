### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given Uber’s current **Grey Zone** status, a balanced turnaround approach is recommended, combining cost containment with strategic investments in core differentiators.

- **Hofer (1980)** emphasizes sequencing turnaround efforts starting with stabilization, then strategic repositioning. Uber should continue stabilizing financials while investing in technology and market expansion.

- **Bibeault (1999)** highlights the importance of diagnosing failure causes; Uber’s past distress likely stemmed from high costs and competitive pressures, now being addressed.

- **Hoskisson et al. (2004)** recommend strategic restructuring focusing on core competencies; Uber’s focus on ride-sharing and delivery aligns with this.

- **Beard (2024)** suggests tech-sector renewal requires innovation and stakeholder alignment, which Uber must prioritize.

- **Freeman (1984)** underscores stakeholder alignment; Uber should engage employees, investors, and partners to support the turnaround.

This approach balances financial discipline with growth initiatives, aiming to push Uber into the Safe Zone over time.
