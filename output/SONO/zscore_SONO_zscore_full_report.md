## Z-Score Formula Used

Z = 1.2*X1 + 1.4*X2 + 3.3*X3 + 0.6*X4 + 1.0*X5
- X1 = (Current Assets - Current Liabilities) / Total Assets
- X2 = Retained Earnings / Total Assets
- X3 = EBIT / Total Assets
- X4 = Equity / Total Liabilities
- X5 = Sales / Total Assets

**Thresholds:**
- Safe Zone: > 2.99
- Grey Zone: > 1.81 and <= 2.99
- Distress Zone: <= 1.81

### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given Sonos’s current **Grey Zone** status, a balanced turnaround approach is recommended. According to Hofer (1980) and Bibeault (1999), the company should sequence retrenchment with strategic investments to stabilize and grow.

- **Cost Containment:** Tighten operational expenses to protect liquidity without sacrificing innovation.  
- **Strategic Investment:** Focus on core product differentiation and R&D to regain market share and improve profitability.  
- **Stakeholder Alignment:** Engage institutional investors and employees to build confidence and support (Freeman, 1984).  
- **Market Repositioning:** Explore partnerships or new market segments to diversify revenue streams (Beard, 2024).

This approach balances risk management with growth potential, appropriate for a company oscillating between Grey and Safe Zones.
