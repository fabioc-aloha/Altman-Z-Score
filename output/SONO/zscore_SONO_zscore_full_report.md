## Z-Score Formula Used

Z = 1.20*X1 + 1.40*X2 + 3.30*X3 + 0.60*X4 + 1.00*X5
- X1 = (Current Assets - Current Liabilities) / Total Assets
- X2 = Retained Earnings / Total Assets
- X3 = EBIT / Total Assets
- X4 = Equity / Total Liabilities
- X5 = Sales / Total Assets

**Thresholds:**
- Safe Zone: > 2.99
- Grey Zone: > 1.81 and <= 2.99
- Distress Zone: <= 1.81

## 2. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Turnaround and renewal theory is critical for Sonos as it navigates moderate financial risk and operational challenges. The Grey Zone status suggests the company is not in immediate distress but must act decisively to avoid deterioration.

- **Strategic Focus:** Following Hofer (1980) and Bibeault (1999), Sonos should prioritize stabilizing operations through cost containment while selectively investing in innovation and product differentiation to regain competitive advantage. This balanced approach aligns with Hoskisson et al. (2004) on strategic restructuring in transitional firms.

- **Innovation & Repositioning:** Beard (2024) emphasizes tech-sector renewal through innovation leadership and stakeholder engagement. Sonos’s Chief Innovation Officer role is pivotal to driving product development and market repositioning.

- **Stakeholder Alignment:** Freeman’s (1984) stakeholder theory underscores the need for transparent communication and alignment with investors, employees, and partners to build trust during change.

- **Management Transition:** The interim CEO appointment suggests a leadership renewal phase. This period should focus on quick wins in operational efficiency and clear strategic messaging to stabilize confidence.

- **Market & Peer Context:** The consumer electronics sector is competitive with rapid innovation cycles. Sonos must leverage its brand and product ecosystem to maintain relevance and margin improvement.

In essence, Sonos’s turnaround should be a phased process balancing retrenchment with strategic renewal, avoiding drastic retrenchment but ensuring disciplined financial management and innovation investment.
