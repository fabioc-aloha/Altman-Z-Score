### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given Carnival’s **Distress Zone** status, turnaround theory emphasizes urgent retrenchment and cash preservation (Hofer, 1980; Bibeault, 1999). The company should prioritize:

- **Retrenchment:** Immediate cost-cutting, asset sales, and operational efficiency improvements to stabilize cash flow.
- **Creditor Negotiations:** Restructuring debt terms to avoid covenant breaches and improve liquidity.
- **Focused Renewal:** Selective investment in core differentiators such as enhanced health protocols and customer experience to regain market confidence.

Freeman’s stakeholder alignment theory (1984) suggests transparent communication with creditors, employees, and customers to maintain trust during restructuring. Hoskisson et al. (2004) recommend strategic restructuring to realign resources with profitable segments.

Recent market optimism and price recovery may provide some breathing room but do not eliminate the need for urgent financial stabilization.
