**Altman Z-Score Calculation (Latest Quarter 2025-04-30):**  
Using the formula for publicly traded manufacturing firms (adapted for retail):  
Z = 1.2X1 + 1.4X2 + 3.3X3 + 0.6X4 + 1.0X5  
Where:  
- X1 = Working Capital / Total Assets = -22.67B / 262.37B = -0.086  
- X2 = Retained Earnings / Total Assets = 90.85B / 262.37B = 0.346

Z = 1.2*(-0.086) + 1.4*0.346 + 3.3*0.0253 + 0.6*4.38 + 1.0*0.631

- **Innovation:** Accelerate digital transformation and e-commerce capabilities, leveraging its existing platforms like Flipkart and PhonePe.  
- **Repositioning:** Enhance customer experience through technology integration and supply chain optimization.  
- **Stakeholder Alignment:** Engage employees, customers, and partners to support strategic initiatives, consistent with Freeman’s (1984) stakeholder theory.

No recent management upheavals or market shocks necessitate retrenchment. Instead, Walmart’s leadership should focus on continuous improvement and strategic investments to maintain market leadership.
