### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given Walmart’s **Safe Zone** status, the focus should be on **strategic renewal and growth** rather than retrenchment. According to Hofer (1980) and Bibeault (1999), firms in strong financial health should emphasize innovation and repositioning to sustain competitive advantage.

Recommendations include:

- **Innovation Investment:** Accelerate digital transformation and e-commerce capabilities to capture shifting consumer behaviors.
- **Stakeholder Alignment:** Engage employees, customers, and partners in sustainability and service excellence initiatives, following Freeman’s (1984) stakeholder theory.
- **Strategic Restructuring:** Pursue selective acquisitions or partnerships to expand market reach, consistent with Hoskisson et al. (2004).

These strategies will help Walmart leverage its financial strength to drive long-term value creation.
