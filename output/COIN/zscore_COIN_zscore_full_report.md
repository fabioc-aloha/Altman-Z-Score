### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given Coinbase’s recent transition from Distress to Grey/Safe Zone, a **balanced turnaround approach** is recommended:

- **Retrenchment and Cash Preservation:** Urgently needed during 2022-early 2024 distress phase, likely involving cost cuts and liquidity management (Hofer, 1980).  
- **Strategic Investment:** As financial health improves, invest selectively in core differentiators like platform innovation and regulatory compliance (Bibeault, 1999).  
- **Stakeholder Alignment:** Engage shareholders, employees, and partners to support growth initiatives and rebuild trust (Freeman, 1984).  
- **Innovation & Repositioning:** Emphasize technology upgrades and market expansion to sustain growth momentum (Beard, 2024).  
- **Governance:** Board oversight should focus on risk management and strategic restructuring to avoid relapse (Hoskisson et al., 2004).

Recent market trends and price rallies support a shift from crisis management to growth enablement.
