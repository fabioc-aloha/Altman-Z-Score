Given PG’s **Safe Zone** status, turnaround theory emphasizes **strategic renewal and growth** rather than retrenchment. According to Hofer (1980) and Bibeault (1999), companies in strong financial health should focus on innovation, market repositioning, and stakeholder alignment to sustain competitive advantage.

PG should leverage its financial strength to invest in R&D, digital transformation, and sustainability initiatives, aligning with Beard (2024) on tech-sector renewal principles adapted for consumer products. No urgent restructuring is needed; instead, PG should pursue strategic initiatives that reinforce its market leadership and adapt to evolving consumer preferences, ensuring continued Z-Score strength and shareholder returns.
