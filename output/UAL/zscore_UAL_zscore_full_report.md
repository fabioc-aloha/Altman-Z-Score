### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given UAL’s **Distress Zone** status, turnaround theory emphasizes urgent retrenchment and cash preservation (Hofer, 1980; Bibeault, 1999). The company should prioritize:

- **Retrenchment:** Immediate cost-cutting, asset sales, and operational streamlining to conserve cash and reduce liabilities.
- **Creditor Negotiations:** Engage proactively with lenders to restructure debt and avoid covenant breaches.
- **Stabilization:** Focus on short-term liquidity stabilization before pursuing growth initiatives.

Freeman’s stakeholder alignment theory (1984) suggests transparent communication with creditors, employees, and shareholders to maintain trust during restructuring.

Given the airline industry’s cyclical nature, strategic renewal (Beard, 2024) should follow once financial stability is restored, focusing on innovation in customer experience and operational efficiency.
