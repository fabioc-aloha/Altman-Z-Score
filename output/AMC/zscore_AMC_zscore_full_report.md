### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given AMC’s placement in the **Distress Zone**, turnaround theory emphasizes urgent retrenchment and cash preservation. According to Hofer (1980) and Bibeault (1999), the company must prioritize:

- **Retrenchment:** Immediate cost-cutting, asset sales, and operational downsizing to conserve cash.
- **Creditor Negotiations:** Engage proactively with lenders to restructure debt and avoid covenant breaches.
- **Stabilization:** Focus on short-term liquidity stabilization before pursuing growth initiatives.

Hoskisson et al. (2004) recommend strategic restructuring focusing on core competencies, which for AMC means optimizing theater operations and exploring alternative revenue streams (e.g., premium experiences). Beard (2024) highlights the importance of technological renewal, suggesting AMC could explore digital innovation in customer engagement.

Freeman’s (1984) stakeholder alignment theory underscores the need to communicate transparently with creditors, employees, and shareholders to maintain trust during the turnaround.
