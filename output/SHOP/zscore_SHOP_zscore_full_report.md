### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given Shopify’s **Safe Zone** status, turnaround theory emphasizes **strategic renewal and growth** rather than retrenchment. According to Hofer (1980) and Bibeault (1999), companies in strong financial health should focus on innovation, market repositioning, and stakeholder alignment to sustain competitive advantage.

Shopify should leverage its strong balance sheet to invest in technology innovation, expand global merchant services, and enhance platform capabilities. Hoskisson et al. (2004) recommend strategic restructuring focused on growth initiatives rather than cost-cutting in such scenarios.

Beard (2024) highlights the importance of continuous renewal in tech firms, suggesting Shopify prioritize R&D and ecosystem partnerships. Freeman’s (1984) stakeholder theory advises aligning internal and external stakeholders around this growth vision to maximize value creation.
