### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given Affirm’s **Distress Zone** status, turnaround theory emphasizes urgent retrenchment and cash preservation (Hofer, 1980; Bibeault, 1999). The company should prioritize:

- **Retrenchment:** Cost cutting, operational efficiency, and halting non-core investments to conserve cash.
- **Creditor Negotiations:** Engage lenders proactively to restructure debt and avoid covenant breaches.
- **Stabilization:** Focus on core BNPL offerings with proven merchant partnerships to restore profitability.
- **Stakeholder Alignment:** Apply Freeman’s (1984) stakeholder theory to maintain trust with investors, creditors, and customers during restructuring.

Recent slight Z-Score improvement suggests initial renewal efforts but the reversion to distress signals incomplete recovery. Strategic restructuring (Hoskisson et al., 2004) should balance retrenchment with selective investments in technology and customer acquisition to regain growth.
