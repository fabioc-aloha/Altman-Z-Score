### 4. Turnaround & Renewal Theory Application

Given Meta’s **Safe Zone** status, turnaround theory emphasizes **strategic renewal and growth** rather than crisis management. According to Hofer (1980) and Bibeault (1999), firms in strong financial health should focus on innovation, market repositioning, and stakeholder alignment to sustain competitive advantage.

Meta should leverage its financial strength to invest in emerging technologies such as augmented reality and AI, consistent with Beard (2024) on tech-sector renewal. Freeman’s (1984) stakeholder theory suggests aligning internal and external stakeholders around a shared vision to drive long-term value.

No urgent retrenchment is needed; instead, Meta should pursue strategic initiatives that reinforce its market leadership and capitalize on growth opportunities while maintaining financial discipline.
